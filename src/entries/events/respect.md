## Description

This week we’ll dive into the topic of respect.
We’ll dive into areas such as whether respect is deserved or earned, if respect must always be mutual, respect towards higher authority, and what does it mean to respect yourself?

## Summary

We dipped into areas such as whether every human deserves to be respected, how we should treat our elders, if respect is a social construct, how giving respect relies heavily on the context, if the feeling of love can be separated from respect, and how we can still respect a person even if we don’t agree with their ideas.

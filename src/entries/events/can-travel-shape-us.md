## Description

This week we’ll ask the question: Can travel shape us? This topic will touch on how new experiences in different places help form our character and personality, how we use travel to find a wider meaning, traveling with an intension to change and whether the real source of change can be achieved without travel.

## Summary

To match the theme of travel, we connected both remotely and in-person from Oslo. We touched on areas such as how travel is not only physical but also mental, how exposing ourselves to different people and cultures can make us question our moral values, the issues facing immigrants who travel to escape war or seeking a better life, and how the true value of travel isn’t about the destination but the journey itself.

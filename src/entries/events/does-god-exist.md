## Description

This week we’ll ask the question: Does God exist? This topic will touch on areas such as the nature of existence, arguments for and against God’s existence, the moral implications of choosing a side, and how the term “God” is perceived across different cultures and religions.

## Summary

We touched on areas such as how collective agreement can lead to existence, whether concepts can exist, does something have to be perceived or labeled by humans to exist, or can it exist on its own?

We also explored different viewpoints of what we call God, ranging from the Christian understanding to the question of whether God could be a shared energy that transcends religious belief.

The topic covered a vast array of subjects that touched on some of the core belief systems we set up for existence and God. We dived into many other side quest discussions along the way but I can only write so much on a summary post.

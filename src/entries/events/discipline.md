## Description

Following on from the topic of resolutions, this week we’ll talk about subject of discipline. We’ll touch on areas such as if discipline come naturally or its something we need to work to obtain, how it shapes our view of freedom, the societal impacts (both positive and negative), and how the power of repetition can morph our behaviours and values.

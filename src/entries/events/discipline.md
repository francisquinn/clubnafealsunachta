## Description

Following on from the topic of resolutions, this week we’ll talk about subject of discipline. We’ll touch on areas such as if discipline come naturally or its something we need to work to obtain, how it shapes our view of freedom, the societal impacts (both positive and negative), and how the power of repetition can morph our behaviours and values.

## Summary

We touched on several areas such as how discipline can be seen as avoiding the instant gratification to strive for the larger goal, how, through our intellectual capacity, we may have lost our innate sense of discipline, how being disciplined for it’s own sake might not lead one to leading a happy life, and how discipline can be used to achieve harmful ends, affecting not only the individual but the broader society around them.

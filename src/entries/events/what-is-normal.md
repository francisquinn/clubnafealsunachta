## Description

This week we’ll explore the question: What is normal?
We’ll dive into areas such as how normalities evolve with time and can change based on society/cultures, who set’s the normal standard, does being normal mean being right, and how does seeking to fit in with social norms affect our quest to discover our authentic selves?

## Summary

We touched on areas such as how normality centres around following the majority, if something is socially accepted it can be considered normal, whether being normal means being right, should minority groups conform to what the majority of a culture consider normal, and how we should be careful when using the word normal in a given context.

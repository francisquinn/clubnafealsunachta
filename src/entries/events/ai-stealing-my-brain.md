## Description

This week we’ll continue the trend of focusing on one question on the topic of ai being–is ai stealing my brain?

This question will focus on whether the use of ai will render our natural cognitive abilities incompetent in the face of an ever growing artificial intelligence and how we should approach using such tools to prevent this from happening.

## Summary

The discussion was varied depending on the definition of “stealing the brain”. We touched on how freeing up cognitive space could further the development of the human race but also the potential downsides and how this could affect society and the future generations.

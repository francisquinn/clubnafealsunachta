## Description

This week will be our first online session. We’ll be exploring the question: Are we present? This topic will focus on where we find ourselves living, either in the now, the future or the past.

## Summary

We touched on areas such as how the present can be seen as a fleeting moment or the current day, how to orient oneself to live in the present while setting plans for the future, and how the distractive nature of social media has affect people to live in the moment.

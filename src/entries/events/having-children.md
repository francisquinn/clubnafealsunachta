## Description

This week we’ll be asking the question of whether we should have children. This question touches on areas such as if we are morally obliged to have children, reasons for not having them, and if it’ ethically just to control procreation.

## Summary

This week we delved into the question of having children. We touched on areas such as the moral duty we have as humans to procreate, the difficulties involved in raising children in our current environment, whether having children should come down to individual choice or for the betterment of society, and if its just that an government can control the birthrate of a country.

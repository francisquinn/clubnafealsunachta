## Description

This week we’ll ask the question does comedy have limits? This question will touch on areas such as if some topics are off-limits, how the intent of a joke can be used for harm or for connection, and how the evolution of comedy shifts through space and time.

## Summary

We looked into how comedy can be used in difficult situations, how the intent of a joke can decide the outcome of it, whether being offended is a choice, and how setting limits to comedy in an ever-changing society can be an extremely cumbersome task.

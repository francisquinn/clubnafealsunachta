## Description

Keeping up with the theme of perfection, this weeks topic we’ll be discussing about the subject of utopia. We’ll touch on whether it’s achievable, the different frameworks put forward by philosophers, ways we could go about attaining it, and whether humans are naturally disposed to ever live in a utopian state.

## Summary

We asked the question of whether it is achievable through collaboration or is a leader required to maintain control. We also talked about how people can be tricked into thinking they’re living in one when in reality the actual society has underlining ethical problem, so whether it could be considered a state of mind which distracts the masses from the reality.

## Description

This week we’ll move away from the topic of artificial intelligence and focus our attention towards the topic of self-awareness. For the first meet-up we’ll discuss the question of how a person can promote a positive or negative change and whether or not the actions of an individual can make an impact on the world.

## Summary

We delved into areas such as how individuals can create communities that could potentially have an impact, whether the choices of an individual are sufficient enough or is a larger infrastructure required, and how we as people can go about bringing about change in our society through demonstrations, strikes and political actions.

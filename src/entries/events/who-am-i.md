## Description

This week we'll continue with the topic of self-awareness with the question: Who am I? This question will touch on areas such as the perception of identity, what makes you you, and how questioning who you are can change the way you view yourself.

## Summary

We started by distinguishing between the mind and body, how one requires the other to form the idea of self, to exploring the african ubuntu philosophy which suggests we all stem from the same source therefore the question of self ceases to exist. We also touched on how the ideas we have of someone can define them, in our own minds, but differs from the opinion of others and even the person in question.

So who am i? as always, we didn’t find the definitive answer, but we sure did have fun trying :)

Welcome to the Club na Fealsúnachta blog!

I'm going to kindly ask you for a few minutes of your day to explain the purpose behind this blog. But before I begin:

!!! quick disclaimer !!!

I'm no philosopher by any means. I’m just someone who has a natural inclination to ask questions and be content with not receiving a straight-forward answer. Some of the questions and answers my future self will compose might be completely off, so please, don’t take what I write to be the bible.

Grand, with that out of the way, I can get back to where I left off.

The main purpose of this blog is to create a platform where I can express my thoughts and feelings on the many different topics and questions within the realm of philosophy that I find interesting. The blog is not only a personal pursuit into the acquisition of knowledge, but it also aims to spark curiosity into discussing the topics and difficult questions that I feel deserve some time in the spotlight.

I don't know how often I’ll post, or whether this project will be worthwhile, so for now, I'll try to keep it as simple as possible then build up from there.

If you have any questions or would like to get in contact with me, feel free to drop me an email or message on any of the socials.

Thank you for reading and have a great day!
